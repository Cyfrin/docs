# Complete a Course Quiz

## Overview

Cyfrin Updraft courses include **quizzes** as optional checkpoints throughout the curriculum. These assessments help learners evaluate their comprehension of course material.

## How Quizzes Work

Quizzes appear among course lessons and serve as self-assessment tools. As explained in the help article, these checkpoints allow you to "test your skills and see how closely you've been following the course content."

## Feedback and Results

Upon completion, the system provides immediate feedback. You receive information about your performance strengths and areas warranting further review. Results display your score (e.g., 80%) alongside constructive guidance.

## Optional Participation

Quizzes are completely optional. The article notes: "feel free to challenge yourself as you go, or come back to the quizzes later." Learning pace and approach remain entirely within your control.
