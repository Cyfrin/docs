# How do I download a PDF copy of my certification?

## Overview
After successfully completing a certification exam, you have two convenient methods to obtain your PDF certificate.

## Method 1: From the Certification Page

1. Visit [updraft.cyfrin.io/certifications](https://updraft.cyfrin.io/certifications)
2. Select your passed certification
3. Click the green "Download Certification" button to retrieve your PDF

## Method 2: From Your Email

1. You'll receive a confirmation email after passing your exam
2. The email contains a link for downloading your certificate as a PDF
3. Follow the provided instructions to access and save your certificate
