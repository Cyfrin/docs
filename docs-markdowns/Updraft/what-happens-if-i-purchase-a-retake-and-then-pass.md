# What happens if I purchase a "retake" and then pass?

## Article Content

If you buy a retake option for your Cyfrin certification exam and pass on your first try, the retake will not be refunded or credited.

"All sales are final, and no refunds are issued once the retake option is purchased, regardless of whether it is used."

The platform recommends carefully assessing your preparation level before purchasing a retake to ensure the purchase aligns with your actual needs.
