---


## Overview

"Cyfrin Profiles are integrated, cross-platform accounts that combine individual Updraft, CodeHawks, and Solodit logins" into a unified experience. These profiles establish a personal space where users can engage across multiple Cyfrin platforms.

## Key Features

The platform offers three main benefits:

1. **Unified Ecosystem** – Cyfrin Profiles connect the three primary platforms, enabling smooth navigation between them.

2. **Single Sign-On** – Users enjoy "one login for all Cyfrin products, enabling new users easy access to the Cyfrin ecosystem."

3. **Customized Account Space** – Users can display their accomplishments and development across Cyfrin's offerings in one location, making it suitable for sharing credentials with colleagues and recruiters.
