## Overview

Coupons offer discounts on certification purchases. The redemption process involves four straightforward steps completed during checkout.

## Steps to Redeem

**1. Select Your Certification**

Visit [updraft.cyfrin.io/certifications](https://updraft.cyfrin.io/certifications) and choose your desired certification. Click "Get Certified" and add any optional add-ons you want.

**2. Proceed to Payment**

After finalizing your selection, click "Proceed to Payment" to advance to the checkout page.

**3. Apply Your Coupon**

On the order summary (right side of screen), enter your coupon code in the input field and select "Apply". The discount will instantly reduce your total.

**4. Important Note**

Even if your coupon covers 100% of the cost, you must still provide credit card information. This requirement stems from limitations by payment processor Stripe.
