## Article Content

You've put in the work and the results speak for themselves! If you've completed a course and are excited to share the achievement, Updraft allows you to do this smoothly!

### Steps to Share Your Achievement

1. **Navigate to the course summary page** of the course you've completed.

2. **Click the Share achievement button** on the course page (as shown in the screenshot of the "Introduction to Python and Vyper" course).

3. **Select your preferred sharing platform** or download your own PNG image to share your accomplishment.

A congratulations pop-up will appear with options to share your completion across various social media platforms or save the certificate as an image file.
