## Overview

Updraft hosts diverse content for all skill levels. The platform helps learners discover appropriate courses through an organized browsing system.

## Finding Courses

To locate courses on the Cyfrin Updraft platform:

1. **Navigate to Courses**: "Click on **Courses** in the top navigation bar."
2. **Browse by Level**: "Select a course, or click **view all courses** for your specific level of skill."

The browsing interface presents beginner, intermediate, and advanced course sections. Selecting "view all courses" displays a comprehensive summary page of available offerings at that difficulty level.

## Enrollment Process

Once you've identified an interesting course:

1. Select **Go to course** to access the course summary page
2. Click **Continue course** to begin from the start or resume your previous progress
