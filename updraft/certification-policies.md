## All Sales Are Final

"All sales are final. Once a purchase has been completed, refunds or cancellations are not permitted." Buyers should thoroughly review their orders before completing payment.

## Voucher Validity

"Each exam voucher is valid for 365 days from the date of purchase." After this expiration window closes, purchasers must buy another voucher to schedule their examination.
